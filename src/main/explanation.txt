this is an internal document explaining how the thing works (poorly)


the statemachine handles all ingoing and outgoing information between the bus and alu



the main alu verilog file contains most of the microcode handling and stuff.
the main alu also holds the native ternary registers as well as the swap registers and the masking registers
swap registers also handles the aliasing of swapping registers
the main alu is the final deciding factor on what to do during a clock cycle
shadow registers are not part of the system


internal bus access passes through the microcode provider before entering the alu normally



some micro ops operate during housekeeping phases
these micro ops do not require feeding, as they are internal operations

housekeeping duties are an extension of the register control, and theres 1 for each thread.

housekeeping operations include

dealiasing data

handling read/write masks
